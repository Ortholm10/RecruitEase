# Full Stack Engineer (Mid-Senior)

**Company:** Northgate Labs
**Location:** Bengaluru (Hybrid) · Full-time

## About the role

We're looking for a Full Stack Engineer to join our core product team. You'll
own features end-to-end — from schema design through to a shipped UI — and
work closely with design and product to define what we build next.

## Requirements

- 4+ years of professional experience building production web applications.
- Strong hands-on experience with **React** (hooks, component architecture,
  performance optimization at scale). This is a must-have.
- Proficient in **Node.js** and **TypeScript** for backend services. This is
  a must-have.
- Solid working knowledge of **SQL and relational databases** (schema design,
  indexing, query optimization) — we use PostgreSQL. This is a must-have.
- Experience designing and consuming **REST or GraphQL APIs**.
- Comfortable with **system design** — you should be able to reason about
  trade-offs when a feature needs to scale.
- Experience writing **automated tests** (unit and integration) as part of
  your normal workflow, not as an afterthought.
- Familiarity with a **cloud platform** (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and basic CI/CD.
- Strong written and verbal **communication skills** — you'll be expected to
  explain technical trade-offs to non-technical stakeholders.

## Nice to have

- Experience with Next.js specifically.
- Exposure to infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, Pulumi).
- Prior experience at an early-stage startup (ambiguity doesn't scare you).

## What you'll do

- Design and ship full features, from database schema to UI.
- Participate in code review and help set engineering standards.
- Debug production issues and contribute to on-call rotation.
- Mentor junior engineers on the team.
